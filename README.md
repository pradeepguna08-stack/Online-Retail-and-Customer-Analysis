Online Retail and Customer Analysis
Online Retail and Customer Analysis,This project focuses on analyzing online retail transaction data to understand sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, and business trends using Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).

Project Objectives:- 
Analyze overall sales performance and monthly sales trends. - Identify top-performing products and regions based on sales. - Examine returns and cancellations to understand their impact on revenue. - Perform RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis to segment customers based on their purchasing behavior. - Generate actionable business insights to support data-driven decision-making. 
Tools & Technologies - Python - Pandas and NumPy - Matplotlib and Seaborn - Jupyter Notebook 

Key Outcomes:-
Explored retail sales patterns, identified important sales trends, and segmented customers using RFM analysis to better understand customer engagement and purchasing behavior.  This project demonstrates practical skills in data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, data visualization, and customer analytics.
